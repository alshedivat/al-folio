I am an Assistant Professor at SUNY Plattsburgh specializing in operations research, business analytics, and data-driven decision-making under uncertainty. My work combines stochastic optimization, simulation, and predictive modeling to support complex decisions in public, environmental, and organizational systems.

I completed my PhD in Industrial Engineering at <a href="https://www.njit.edu/academics/major/industrial-engineering">New Jersey Institute of Technology</a> under the supervision of <a href="https://soml.ise.vt.edu/">Dr. Esra Buyuktahtakin Toy</a>. My dissertation focused on multistage stochastic optimization and reinforcement learning models for invasive species management and epidemic control planning.

In teaching, I emphasize practical analytics and reproducible methods that students can apply immediately in industry and research. I also mentor student projects that connect statistical and optimization tools to real-world decision problems. I am always open to collaborations on high-impact research and applied analytics projects.
