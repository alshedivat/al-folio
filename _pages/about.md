I'm Karan.

I think too much. About systems, about people, about why things are the way they are, and whether they ever could be different. I am not always sure it helps.

My work sits somewhere around tech and people. I've worked with teams, I try to write and break into code, and spent more hours than I'd like to admit reading about things that have nothing to do with any of it. Maybe that's just what happens when curiosity takes over, or plain stubbornness. I haven't decided which.

Right now I'm trying to learn, trying to build, figuring things out. Not because I have it all mapped. Just because moving feels more honest than staying still, even when I don't know where I'm going.

This is where I share what I'm learning.
