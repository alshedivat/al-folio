My name [**Ge**](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E8%88%B8) [**Yu**](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E4%BD%99) is pronounced roughly as _"guh yoo"_ — closer to the "g" in _go_ than the "g" in _gem_, and the "yu" rhymes with _you_.

In Mandarin (汉语拼音), it is written **Gě Yú** (with third-tone _Gě_ and second-tone _Yú_).

Feel free to call me **Ge** for short.
