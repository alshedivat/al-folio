# al-folio Showcase

The vibrant community of **al-folio** users is growing! Academics around the world use al-folio for their homepages, blogs, and lab pages, as well as webpages for courses, workshops, conferences, meetups, and more.

## Add your site

We no longer accept pull requests that add entries to this list. Instead, **post a request in the _Showcase_ category of [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/alshedivat/al-folio/discussions)** with a link to your site and which group it belongs in (Academics, Labs, Courses, or Conferences & workshops). Requests are reviewed and added in batches, so it may take a little while before your entry appears here — thanks for your patience.
