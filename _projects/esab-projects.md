### About the Project
My time with ESAB gave me a valuable experience on the product design cycle. I started with the product engineers, working through how formulations and prototypes pass tests even before they are fabricated. I walked through the various classifications and requirements new products need to pass, and assisted with the materials science applications of each design.

I then spent time with Engineering Technicians, actually producing the different prototypes I had helped design. I was able to assist with both electrode production and flux-cored wire fabrication.

Finally, I worked with test welders to see how the prototypes held up in field-like conditions. While the potential product failed some tests, the addition of different raw material created a 20% increase in the overall weldability of the electrode. I then graded the welds and the overall product, and brought that criteria back to the product engineers to begin the cycle again.
