### About the Project
In November of 2025, my roommates and I complained to each other about our class schedules for the semester, and how much time it took to research the best courses and professors each year. From there, we started on Declassify, our AI powered student schedule builder.

This website uses APIs to call all the data from the CU Boulder Faculty Course Questionnaires, the CU course catelog, Reddit, ratemyprofessor.com, and the professor descriptions on CU's website. This data, combined with the AI agent, allow for quick and easy schedule building.

I was primarily responsible for funding pitches and investor outreach, as well as assisting on UI development. Our website is currently undergoing final testing pending an initial release in late-February.
