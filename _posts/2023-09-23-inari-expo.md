Participated as __a finalist of technology and engineering sciences cluster__ in Indoensia Research and Innovation Fair organized by the National Research and Innovation Agency of Indonesia (BRIN) and received __a special award__ recognition from PT Nanotech Indonesia Global Tbk.
    
The event was held on September 20-23, 2023 at the ICC Building, Science and Technology area Soekarno, Cibinong, Jawa Barat, Indonesia. During the process I presented my research work to four nanotechnology and materials scientists in Indonesia, sharing and communicating it to the public (including graduate and undergraduate students from many universities in Indonesia) through a three-minute research talk (3MT) and scientific poster.
