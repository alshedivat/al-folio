Adlerian psychology presented as a Socratic dialogue between a philosopher and a frustrated young man. The core argument — that we choose our own suffering, that trauma doesn't determine us, and that freedom means being disliked — is genuinely confronting. One of the few self-help books that actually changed how I think.
