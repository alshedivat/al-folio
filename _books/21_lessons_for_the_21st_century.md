Where Sapiens looked backwards and Homo Deus looked forward, this one sits squarely in the present — technology, politics, religion, meaning. Harari doesn't pretend to have answers, but the questions he raises are the ones everyone should be thinking about. One of those rare books that genuinely shifts how you read the news.
