A simple story about a shepherd boy chasing a recurring dream across the desert — and finding that the journey itself is the treasure. The prose is deceptively plain, but the ideas about following your Personal Legend have a way of resurfacing at the right moments. Easy to read in a weekend, hard to stop thinking about.
